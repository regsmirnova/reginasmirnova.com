Regina Smirnova

Product Designer

I design how people communicate and express themselves on new devices, from the first sketch to launch.

For the last four years I was at Meta, most recently at WhatsApp, designing for Meta VR Glasses, photorealistic avatar calling, and Meta's first AI-powered avatar editor. Before that, I worked on consumer apps at Revolut, Bumble and Shares, and started out at Yandex.

Outside work, I sew my own clothes and recently finished a fashion design course at Central Saint Martins. This summer I built one app a week with AI and shared the process on Instagram.
